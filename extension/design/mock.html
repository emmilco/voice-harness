Why do we divide by the square root of the head size here?
📍 model.py:52-54

Here we see the raw attention scores: every query dotted with every key. Notice that nothing bounds their size yet, and that matters for the next line.

This is the division by sqrt(d_k). With 64 dimensions, each dot product is a sum of 64 terms, so its typical size grows with the square root of 64. Dividing puts the scores back on a scale the softmax can work with.

Without that scaling the softmax saturates: one key gets almost all the weight and the gradients through the others vanish. The paper calls this out in a footnote, and it’s easy to see in a plot.
